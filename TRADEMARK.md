# Tradr Trademark Policy

## Summary

The **Tradr software is free and open source** under the Apache License, Version 2.0. You can use it, run it,
study it, modify it, and redistribute it under that license.

The **Tradr name and logo are trademarks** of MOSSFOOT DIGITAL INC. Trademarks are **not** covered by the Apache-2.0 license — in fact Apache-2.0 explicitly grants no
trademark rights (Section 6). This policy explains how you may and may not use the Tradr name and logo.

The goal is simple: **let everyone use the software freely, while making sure users can always tell what is
the official Tradr project and hosted service and what is not.**

## Uses that do NOT require permission

You may, without asking us:

- **Refer to the software by name** — in articles, tutorials, reviews, talks, comparisons, and documentation
  (e.g. "a guide to self-hosting Tradr," "migrating from a spreadsheet to Tradr").
- **State compatibility or integration truthfully** — "works with Tradr," "built on the Tradr API,"
  "imports into Tradr" — as long as it does not imply that we produce, sponsor, or endorse your product.
- **Redistribute unmodified official builds** under the Tradr name (keeping the name, notices, and this
  policy intact).
- **Run your own instance** of Tradr (hosted for yourself, your team, or your company) — self-hosting is a
  first-class use and needs no permission.

When you use the name referentially, use it as an adjective followed by the generic term where natural
("the Tradr journal," "the Tradr project"), don't alter the spelling or styling, and don't make "Tradr" the
most prominent element of your own branding.

## Uses that require our permission — or are not allowed

Without prior written permission, you may **not**:

- Use **"Tradr"** (or any confusingly similar name) as, or as part of, **your product name, company name,
  service name, app name, domain name, or social-media handle**.
- Offer a **modified version** of the software, or a **hosted/managed service**, under the name "Tradr" or
  any confusingly similar name, or in a way that suggests it is the official offering.
- Use the **Tradr logo** except to link to the official project, unmodified, without implying endorsement.
- Use the name or logo in a way that **implies official status, affiliation, sponsorship, or endorsement**
  by the Tradr project when none exists.
- Register a **domain name** containing "Tradr" (or a confusing variant) for a product or service.

## Forks

You are free to fork the Apache-2.0 code. If you distribute or operate a **modified** version, you must:

1. **Choose a different name** that is not "Tradr" and is not confusingly similar, and
2. **Remove the Tradr logo and Tradr branding** from your distribution, and
3. Not present your fork as the official Tradr or imply endorsement.

You may still state, factually, that your project is "based on Tradr" or "a fork of Tradr."

## Hosted service

Only the official project (and those it authorizes in writing) may offer a hosted service **called Tradr**
or operated at **tradr.cloud** or a confusingly similar domain. Anyone hosting a fork as a service for others
must do so under a different name, per the Forks section above. (Running Tradr for your own organization's
internal use is fine and is not a "hosted service" under this policy.)

## Logo

The Tradr logo files, when provided, are made available for referential and link-back use only. Do not
modify the logo's colors, proportions, or elements; do not combine it with your own logo or use it as a
component of another logo; do not use it as a favicon or app icon for a non-official product.

## Requesting permission

For any use not clearly permitted above — including partnerships, co-branding, merchandise, or a hosted
offering — contact **legal@tradr.cloud**. We're friendly and want the community
to thrive; this policy exists to prevent user confusion, not to stop legitimate use.

## Changes

We may update this policy as the project grows. The current version always lives in this file in the
official repository.
