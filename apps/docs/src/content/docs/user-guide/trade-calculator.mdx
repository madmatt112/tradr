---
title: 'Use the trade calculator'
description: 'From entry, target, and stop, get position size, R:R, dollar risk, and estimated fees, sized against a chosen account balance before you enter.'
---

The trade calculator sizes a trade before you take it. Open it from the
**Calculator** link in the sidebar.

Nothing on this page is submitted or saved. The calculator recomputes as you
type, so you can try a setup, change a number, and read the new result straight
away. The form is on the left, the results are on the right.

## Direction and mode

**Direction** is a two-tab choice: **Long** or **Short**.

**Mode** is a two-tab choice: **Stock** or **Options**. The field below the mode
changes with it.

## Pick the instrument

Under **Stock**, the **Symbol** field takes a ticker. When your instance has a
stock-quote provider configured, a **Pull last price** button appears once you
have typed a symbol. It fills **Entry price** with the last price, which is about
15 minutes delayed.

Under **Options**, **Select from options chain** opens the chain. Pick a contract
to use its premium as the entry price. When the contract has no last trade, you
enter the premium by hand instead.

## The trade prices

Three price fields drive the size:

- **Entry price** - the price you plan to get in at.
- **Stop loss** - the price at which you accept the trade is wrong. The distance
  from entry to stop is the **Per-unit risk** in the results, and the position
  size comes from it: a wider stop buys fewer units for the same money at risk.
- **Target price (optional)** - leave it out and you still get a size. Add it and
  the results also show the **Per-unit reward** and the **Risk/Reward ratio**.

## Risk

**Risk** is a two-tab choice for how you say the amount you are willing to lose:

- **Dollar** shows one field, **Dollar risk**, which is a figure you type.
- **Percent** shows **Balance** and **Risk percent**, and sizes the risk from the
  balance and the percentage.

## Account

**Account** picks the account you are trading. Under **Percent** it supplies the
balance to size against, and in either basis it supplies the display currency and
caps the position at the buying power the account has.

When the account carries a default risk rule, choosing it prefills
**Risk percent**. The change applies to this calculation only: the account keeps its
rule. An account with a brokerage attached also brings that brokerage's fee
schedule into the estimate, which you can still change or clear under **Fees**.

## Fees

**Fees** is a three-tab choice:

- **None** adds no fees.
- **Brokerage** sizes fees from a selected brokerage's schedule.
- **Manual** shows a **Manual fees** field you type.

## Results

The results panel stays empty until the prices and one risk basis are complete.

**Position Sizing** gives you:

- **Position size** - whole units, rounded down.
- **Per-unit risk** - the entry-to-stop distance per unit.
- **Actual dollar risk** - the risk once the size is rounded down, so it lands at
  or under your budget.
- **Total position value**.

When the account's buying power is the limit, the panel marks the size with a
**Buying power** note.

**Risk / Reward** appears only with a target. It adds the **Per-unit reward** and
the **Risk/Reward ratio** - what the plan pays if it works, per unit of what it
costs if it does not.

**Fee Impact** appears when fees are on. It shows the **Estimated fees**, the
**Fee-to-risk %**, the **Adjusted dollar risk**, and the **Breakeven**.
**Adjusted Risk / Reward** then restates the ratio after fees.

In **Percent** mode, **Derived Dollar Risk** shows the dollar figure the
percentage worked out to.
