---
title: 'Customise the dashboard'
description: 'Add, remove, and drag-drop widgets — stats, open positions, equity curve, account balances, position sizing — with a layout that persists per user.'
---

The dashboard is a grid of widgets you arrange to suit you. Open it from the
**Dashboard** link in the sidebar. Your layout is saved per user, so it is there
again next time you sign in.

## Add a widget

Choose **Add Widget** above the grid. The picker lists every widget not already
on the dashboard. Click one to place it. When every widget is already on the
grid, the picker shows **All widgets added.**

The widgets are:

- **Stats Summary**
- **Open Positions**
- **Performance Chart**
- **Account Balances**
- **Position Sizing**
- **Equity Curve**

## Remove a widget

Each widget has a menu button in its header. Open it and choose **Remove**. The
widget leaves the grid and goes back into the **Add Widget** picker.

## Move and resize

On a pointer device, drag a widget by its header to move it, and drag an edge or
a corner to resize it. The widgets around it give way as you drag. The grid saves
the new layout when you let go.

On a touch device the dashboard stacks the widgets in a single column and does not
let you drag or resize.

## Reset the layout

Choose **Reset layout** above the grid. A **Reset dashboard layout** dialog asks
you to confirm: every widget goes back to its default position and size, and any
widget you removed comes back. Your trading data is not affected.
Choose **Reset layout** to confirm, or **Cancel** to keep your current layout.
