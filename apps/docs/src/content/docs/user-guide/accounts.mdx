This page explains brokerage accounts (an **account** in Tradr), their cash
balance, and the ledger behind it. It covers deposits, withdrawals,
reconciliation, and the multi-currency total on the dashboard.

An account mirrors a real brokerage account: one currency, one starting balance,
and the positions you trade in it. Tradr never connects to your broker. You
record what you have already done.

## Create an account

Every position, fill and ledger entry books against an account. You need one
before anything else works.

Open **Accounts**, then choose **New Account**. The form has six fields:
**Name**, **Currency**, **Trading-day timezone**, **Starting balance**,
**Default risk %**, and **Brokerage**. [Getting started, step
2](/user-guide/getting-started/#step-2--create-a-brokerage-account) describes
each one.

Each account holds one currency. It fixes the currency for this account's
balance, fees and P&L. To track a second currency, add another account.

## The starting balance

The starting balance is the account's opening cash. It is the baseline every
later figure measures against.

Set it once, in the account form. The balance Tradr shows is this figure plus
every ledger entry. Leave it empty and the account starts at zero.

You cannot change the starting balance after the account exists. Changing it
would move every past balance with it. To correct the cash instead, record a
deposit or withdrawal, or reconcile the balance.

## Record a deposit or withdrawal

Record a deposit or withdrawal for cash you moved into or out of the account.
Tradr adds one ledger entry in the account's currency and moves the balance by
the amount.

1. Open the account from **Accounts**.
2. On the **Balance** card, choose **Deposit / withdrawal**.
3. Set **Type** to **Deposit** or **Withdrawal**.
4. Enter the **Amount** in the account's currency.
5. Set the **Date and time**.
6. Choose **Record deposit** or **Record withdrawal**.

The dialog previews the **Current balance** and the **Resulting balance** as you
type.

**Expected result:** the balance moves by the amount. A new **Deposit** or
**Withdrawal** row appears in the ledger.

A withdrawal larger than the balance is accepted. Tradr shows a warning that the
balance will go below zero, then records the movement anyway. The balance is
cash only. It does not include the market value of open positions.

## Delete a deposit or withdrawal

Delete a deposit or withdrawal you recorded by mistake. Tradr reverses it and
keeps a record.

1. Find the **Deposit** or **Withdrawal** row in the ledger.
2. Choose the delete button at the end of that row.
3. Confirm in the dialog.

Tradr does not erase the row. It adds a reversal entry for the same amount and
keeps the original. Both rows stay in the ledger. The reversal carries a
**(reversal)** badge, and the balance returns to what it was before.

Only a deposit or withdrawal row has a delete button. A reversal row has none. To
change an amount, delete the movement, then record it again with the right
figure.

## Reconcile the cash balance

Reconcile the balance when Tradr's figure and your broker's cash figure
disagree. Tradr posts one adjusting entry for the difference.

1. Open the account from **Accounts**.
2. On the **Balance** card, choose **Reconcile**.
3. Enter the **Actual cash balance** the account should show.
4. Choose **Post adjustment**.

**Expected result:** a **Balance adjustment** row appears in the ledger for the
difference. The balance now matches the figure you entered.

Reconcile against cash only. Tradr's balance is the starting balance plus
realized P&L from closed positions. It does not include the market value of open
positions.

The **Balance** card shows two more lines when you hold open positions: **Cash**
and **Positions**. The **Positions** figure is the cost basis of those
positions, not their market value. Tradr has no quote source, so it shows cost
basis only.

## Read the ledger

Below the **Balance** card, the account page shows its **Ledger**. The ledger
lists every entry that moved the balance, newest first.

The ledger has these columns:

| Column          | Meaning                                           |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| **Occurred at** | The date and time the entry happened.             |
| **Position**    | A label, or a link to the position it belongs to. |
| **Debit**       | Money out of the account.                         |
| **Credit**      | Money into the account.                           |
| **Balance**     | The running balance after this entry.             |

Every entry is one of five kinds:

| Row                | Meaning                                                          |
| ------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Trade P&L          | Realized P&L from a position. The **Position** cell links to it. |
| Reversal           | Reverses an earlier entry. It carries a **(reversal)** badge.    |
| Balance adjustment | The entry a reconcile posts.                                     |
| Deposit            | Cash you added to the account.                                   |
| Withdrawal         | Cash you removed from the account.                               |

The **Balance** column is a running total. It shows the account balance after
each entry, down the page.

The ledger shows 50 rows per page. Use **Previous** and **Next** to move between
pages. The running balance stays correct on every page. Tradr anchors each page
to the balance at its top row.

## Display currency and exchange rates

Each account holds one currency. Tradr supports accounts in different currencies
and consolidates them on the dashboard.

Set your **display currency** under **Settings → Profile**. This is the currency
the dashboard uses for its cross-currency total. Per-account balances stay in
their own currency.

Tradr does not fetch exchange rates. You enter them by hand. On **Settings →
Profile**, use **Exchange Rates** to add a rate for a currency pair. Give the
**Base currency**, the **Quote currency**, the **Rate**, and an **Effective
date**. Choose **Save rate**.

The dashboard's **Total (all accounts)** widget sums every account into your
display currency. It appears only when you hold accounts in more than one
currency.

The total shows a dash when a rate is missing. Point at the dash to see which
currency pairs need a rate. Choose **Enter rate** to add the missing rate on the
settings page.
