This page tells you what happens when you delete your Tradr account. It lists
what Tradr removes, what stays, and why. Read it before you delete your account.

The account control is in **Settings**, on the **Account** tab, below the log-out
control.

## What deletion removes and what it keeps

### When deletion runs

Deletion is permanent. Tradr keeps no soft copy, and you cannot restore the
account after deletion runs.

If you have no paid period to finish, deletion runs immediately. If you have a
paid subscription, Tradr schedules the deletion for the end of the current paid
period. You keep the account until then. You can cancel a scheduled deletion at
any time before it runs.

Once deletion runs, it is immediate and permanent.

### What stays

Some records stay after deletion. They do not hold your journal data, but you
must know that they exist:

- Stripe keeps your customer record and your invoices. Stripe is the payment
  processor.
- `webhook_events` rows stay. They hold no user id.
- `admin_audit_log` rows stay. Tradr sets the user reference to null and puts a
  hash marker in place of the email address.
- One tombstone row stays, in the `account_deletions` table. It holds the user
  id, a random id, an email hash, the plan tier, the date, who started the
  deletion, and the result of the data purge. This row lets Tradr refuse a
  second deletion of the same account.
- [Backups](/self-hosting/backup-restore/) keep a copy of the account until each
  backup expires.

Tradr computes the email hash on the server, and the hash never leaves the
server in a response. The hash has no salt. Thus a person who knows the email
address can match that address to the tombstone.

### Your money

Tradr gives no refund for a deletion.

If Tradr schedules the deletion, you keep the account and the paid
[tier](/user-guide/reference/plan-limits/) until the period ends. Tradr does not
refund the unused time.

Unused wallet credits are deleted with the account. Tradr does not refund them.

### Analytics and email

After the deletion commits, Tradr deletes the matching person record in PostHog,
the analytics service. This step is best-effort: it runs after the account is
already gone. If the step fails, Tradr writes a log entry and the deletion still
stands. A failure never blocks or reverses the deletion.

Tradr does not send a confirmation email for a deletion. Emails that Tradr sent
before the deletion can stay in the email provider's delivery logs. The
provider's own retention rule controls how long they stay.

### Self-hosted instances

Tradr is self-hostable. On a self-hosted instance, the operator holds all of
this data.

The Stripe, object storage, PostHog, and email lines above apply only where the
operator [configures](/self-hosting/optional-integrations/) that service. Where a
service is not configured, its records do not exist.

A restored backup that is older than the deletion brings the account back. The
restored account does not carry its tombstone, so Tradr treats it as a normal
account again.
