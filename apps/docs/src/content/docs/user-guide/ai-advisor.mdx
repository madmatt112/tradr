Start a conversation, pick a provider/model and persona, upload chart images, and
opt in to trade-data access so the advisor can reason about your own positions.

:::note[Not on the hosted app yet]
The advisor is switched off on app.tradr.cloud while it is reworked. It is not in
the navigation or in Settings there, and it is not part of the Free or Pro plan
until it returns. Self-hosted instances have it today. An operator can withdraw
it on any instance with `DISABLE_ADVISOR=true`.
:::

<HostedOnly>
  On the hosted app you can either **bring your own key (BYOK)** or spend **wallet
  credits** billed through your account. On a self-hosted instance you always
  supply your own LLM key.
</HostedOnly>

:::note
The step-by-step walkthrough — choosing a provider and model, the personas, image
uploads, and the trade-data opt-in — is not written yet.
:::
